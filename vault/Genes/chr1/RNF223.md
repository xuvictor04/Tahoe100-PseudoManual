*`cell-cycle shift`: the drug-dose moved its cells into or out of S or G2/M. Its alert says how much of this gene's change that shift would explain (`cc_share`), from the gene's own slopes on the S-phase and G2/M modules (`tables/genes.parquet`: -0.13 per unit of S-phase module lfc, +0.51 per unit of G2/M module lfc).

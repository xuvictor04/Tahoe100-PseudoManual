**Function**, from UniProt [Q8NBI2](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8NBI2) (Lysosomal membrane ascorbate-dependent ferrireductase CYB561A3):

> Transmembrane reductase that uses ascorbate as an electron donor in the cytoplasm and transfers electrons across membranes to reduce iron cations Fe(3+) into Fe(2+) in the lumen of the late endosome and lysosome. Reduced iron can then be extruded from the late endosome and lysosome to the cytoplasm by divalent metal-specific transporters. It is therefore most probably involved in endosomal and lysosomal cellular iron homeostasis.

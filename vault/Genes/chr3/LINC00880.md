# LINC00880

*long independently transcribed non-coding RNA 880* — 3q25.31

> [!summary] In one line
> LINC00880 is a long intergenic non-coding RNA on chromosome 3, known only from a handful of single-laboratory cancer studies.

## What it is

The annotation is sparse: a lincRNA locus on chromosome 3, few isoforms, no protein product, and no evident sequence conservation — which matters, because conservation is one of the few prior arguments for a lncRNA being functional. On the functional side there are three cancer reports, each in a different tumour type and each proposing a different mechanism: overexpression accelerating colorectal tumour growth with cell-cycle and DNA-replication genes implicated downstream; a super-enhancer-driven transcript in lung adenocarcinoma said to scaffold CDK1 and PRDX1 together; and promotion of proliferation and invasion in spinal ependymoma through a MAPK route. None has been independently replicated and they do not converge. There is no knockout model and no established interaction partner.

## What is uncertain

Nearly everything. Each published mechanism rests on overexpression or knockdown in one laboratory's cell models — the level at which many proposed lncRNA mechanisms have not survived scrutiny. They cannot all be the gene's primary function, and possibly none is. Low-abundance intergenic transcripts are also where quantification is least reliable, so part of the signal may be unspliced or ambient RNA. With no settled function there is nothing to judge a correlation against. Treat any association it shows as worth a targeted test, not a result.

## References

- Feng et al., 2023, Cell Death and Disease — super-enhancer-driven LINC00880 proposed to scaffold CDK1 and PRDX1 in lung adenocarcinoma.
- Gao et al., 2025, Translational Cancer Research — LINC00880 overexpression promotes colorectal cancer growth, with cell-cycle and DNA-replication genes downstream.

The spinal ependymoma report described above is real but reaches me only through a database listing, so no citation is given for it.

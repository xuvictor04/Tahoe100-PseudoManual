# MG53 (TRIM72) in Tahoe-100M: findings from the full run

This is a review of your full run (`mg53_setup.sh --mode full`) over Tahoe-100M: 50 pooled cancer cell lines, 379 drugs at 0.05 / 0.5 / 5 uM, 14 plates, mRNA read 24 h after treatment. Four areas were read (reproduction, upstream of MG53, downstream of MG53, exported data), and an independent verifier checked each one. Only verified claims and numbers are used here. Date: 2026-11-02.

Scope. Two questions only: what raises or lowers MG53 expression (upstream of MG53), and what MG53's level
does to p53 and its output (downstream of MG53). Whether p53 regulates MG53 is NOT in scope and is not
reported; results resting on it, including the cardiac-glycoside lead, have been removed. Separately,
'readthrough spillover' below is a fact about TRIM72's position on the chromosome (it sits 3' of FUS and
PYCARD-AS1 on the same strand, so transcription running past them is counted as TRIM72) and about the
measurement -- never a claim that anything regulates anything.

How to read the sources
- Brackets give the file, relative to `mg53_p53/` in your results, then the row, column or key. "row N" is a 0-based data row (the header is not counted). "line N" is a line of a text file. Files that exist only in the repository are marked "repository".
- "recomputed" marks a number that the review scripts derived from the table named, rather than one printed in it. The headline tables are regenerated by `mg53_p53/findings/make_tables.py` in this repository.
- Basis labels. Data-defined: genome-wide or derived from Tahoe, with no curated list. List-based: rests on a curated list (the 21-gene P53_ACTIVITY / P53_SCORE, gene_modules.csv, CollecTRI / DoRothEA regulons, curated or Tahoe MoA drug classes, the 116-gene single-cell panel, the driver-mutation list). Mixed: both.
- Units. Log2 fold change: +1 = 2x. CPM: counts per million. UMI: unique molecule count. q: Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate. A "profile" is one cell line in one well. WT: TP53 wild-type (17 lines). Altered: TP53-altered (33 lines, of which 30-32 enter the models).
- Every TRIM72-p53 relation below is an association in 24 h mRNA. Tahoe has no MG53 perturbation and no protein data.

Terms used throughout
- Readthrough: transcription that runs on past the end of a gene into the DNA 3' of it. TRIM72 sits on chr16p11.2 immediately 3' of FUS and PYCARD-AS1, on the same strand. This is a statement about position on the chromosome, not about regulation. FUS ends 17 kb upstream of TRIM72 and is far more highly expressed (252 vs about 4 CPM) [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_DoG_gene_exposure_classes.csv, row TRIM72].
- Readthrough-geometry ("sense-exposed") gene: a low-expressed gene that lies just 3' of a strongly expressed gene on the same strand, as TRIM72 does.
- Readthrough index: for each drug-dose, the level-matched rise of all readthrough-geometry genes in the genome [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_DoG_index_by_drugdose.csv].
- Placebo exposure: a gene matched to TRIM72 (on expression, or on how much it moves within lines) that is put in TRIM72's place in the same model. It shows what an unremarkable gene gives.
- DD_lfc: the atlas's corrected log2 fold change for a drug-dose. It is the median over lines of each line's log ratio against the plate's drug-well median, corrected for well position and composition. u_dmso: the uncorrected log ratio against the plate's two DMSO wells.

## Bottom line

- Reproduction (strong; computational). Your run reproduces the author's committed tables. Of 170 tables, 125 are identical, 23 close, 22 'differ' and 0 are missing, and all 12 summary tables are byte-identical [compare_with_repo.txt; summary/]. Every 'differs' traces to well order, seeded random draws, floating point, the pandas / Python version, a fresh HGNC download or one degenerate row. No short answer or headline number changes. genome_wide and export, which hold the list-free base, have no committed tables to compare against.
- What raises TRIM72 (strong for two drug-doses, untestable for the rest; data-defined). Idarubicin 5 uM and homoharringtonine 5 uM raise TRIM72 on both of their plates: 12.2x and 10.3x corrected, 6.0x and 5.4x from pooled counts [export/upstream/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv, DD_lfc; upstream_perturbations/tables/a_TRIM72_plate_batch_checks.csv, row 'significant increases: spearman corrected log2FC vs pseudocount-free pooled log2 fold']. Tahoe's own DESeq2 agrees. TRIM72 is one of 11,472 and 11,993 genes these two drugs change [upstream_perturbations/tables/c_TRIM72_specificity_in_raisers.csv]. They are also the only 2 of the 21 drug-doses the calling rule returns that survive a replication requirement: 17 rest on a single used well and cannot be tested at any effect size, mitoxantrone 5 uM passes in 1 of its 2 wells with the two wells opposite in sign, and adagrasib 0.05 uM in 1 of 14. An earlier version of this bullet graded six of the single-well calls 'supported'; they are untestable, not supported. No lowerer is solid.
- How (strong as an association; data-defined). TRIM72 rises together with its same-strand neighbourhood 3' of FUS (readthrough spillover): PYCARD-AS1 correlates with it at r 0.72 across drug-doses [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_locus_neighbours_effects.csv]. It also tracks the genome-wide readthrough index (Spearman 0.45) [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_DoG_tests.csv]. No raiser shows TRIM72-promoter activation, but gene-level 3' counts cannot separate readthrough from promoter firing. Homoharringtonine blocks translation, so its extra mRNA cannot mean more MG53.
- Line traits and p53 (strong; data-defined). A line's untreated TRIM72 follows its own locus: PYCARD-AS1 is its top partner across lines (rho 0.82, rank 1 of 46,775 genes). TP53 status is not examined as a driver here (out of scope) [upstream_coregulation/tables/b_baseline_coexpression_genomewide.csv; b_TRIM72_baseline_tests.csv].
- Downstream p53, list-free (strong null, limited power; data-defined). Over 1,132 drug-doses x 47 lines, TRIM72 has no TP53-WT-specific association with the Tahoe-derived p53 program. The WT-minus-altered slope is -0.0018 log2 per doubling of TRIM72 (95% CI -0.0052 to +0.0016; TP53-label permutation p 0.30), in the middle of 200 placebo genes [genome_wide/tables/c_program_models.csv, row 0]. The test excludes effects above about 0.4% per doubling of measured TRIM72. The matching bound on *true* TRIM72 is withdrawn: an earlier version gave about 1.4% by dividing the measured bound by a split-half reliability of 0.289, and exposure and outcome are counted from the same cells in the same well against the same total-UMI denominator, so that division has no estimand (see 'Power'). The measured bound is the one that stands.
- Downstream p53, list-based (moderate; list-based). The curated 21-gene p53 score agrees on the main point: WT-minus-altered -0.0032, p 0.24 [two_way/tables/key_results.csv, row 0], and no genotype dependence in single cells. It also carries a small negative lean that the data-defined program does not share (lowest 1.8% of random 21-gene sets). Negative slopes in subsets of active drug-doses are not robust.
- Broad downstream (strong null; data-defined). Within drug-doses, 4,500 genes co-vary with TRIM72, but placebo genes give similar counts. No gene differs by TP53 genotype, and the top partners are readthrough-geometry genes [genome_wide/tables/c_calibration.csv; c_screen_genes.csv.gz]. In 24 h mRNA, TRIM72 has no detectable downstream program of its own.

### What the comparison cannot vouch for

- The tool's verdicts can mislead. It reports a row swap as a large numeric difference (the '1.5' is swapped medians). It inflates machine-precision gaps through its 1e-12 floor. It skips every .csv.gz table, and when any text column differs it stops before checking the numbers [repository setup/compare_with_repo.py lines 42-78].
- genome_wide and export commit no tables, so the Tahoe p53 program, the TRIM72-vs-every-gene screen and the export CSVs have no reference. Their inputs are checked, though:
  - the genome_wide tensor equals the atlas W1 on all 14 plates (max difference 0), W2 to 4.8e-7 over 22,118,547 values, and the committed idarubicin per-line TRIM72 to 5.4e-7 (48 lines) [genome_wide/tables/checks.csv rows 2-20];
  - the W1 check passes 42 times in the log, in three separate streaming passes [run_mg53_p53.log];
  - the exported TRIM72 values match the committed ranking for all 1,132 drug-doses (max |DD_lfc - log2FC| 5e-6) [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv vs upstream_perturbations/tables/a_TRIM72_drugdose_ranking.csv; recomputed].

  What remains unchecked across builds is the derivation step: the permutations, folds and gene selection.
- Seeded random pieces reproduce only on an identical build. The seed applied to each run's own row order recreates each run's cap-0 sample exactly (1,194 vs 1,179 groups) [single_cell_all/tables/groups.csv.gz; recomputed].
- The run spans more than one checkout. single_cell_all was rerun after the guard fix (5ad5cec), and export was rerun after a Parquet buffer overflow (843211f) [run_mg53_p53.log lines 1329 and 1913]. Neither change touches the compared tables.

## Upstream: what changes TRIM72 mRNA

### Drugs that raise it (data-defined)

The DESeq2 column gives the share of line-plates that rise at padj < 0.1 in Tahoe's own DESeq2 (recomputed from [export/tahoe_deseq2/TRIM72_deseq2.csv]), with the plate-centred q where relevant. Tahoe's uncentred DESeq2 values sit below 0 on 13 of the 14 plates (see "Using the exported data"; corrected from "every plate" -- plate 9 is positive, and it carries five single-well raisers), so these shares understate rises. The other columns come from [export/upstream/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv] (DD_lfc, DD_z, u_dmso_median_lines_mean) unless noted.

The committed grading never asked the one question that separates these rows: does the call replicate in a
second well? 1,024 of 1,132 drug-doses rest on one used well, so for 17 of the 21 the rule returns,
replication cannot be tested at any effect size; the Reading column below now says so. Only 4 are testable,
and 2 pass: idarubicin 5 uM and homoharringtonine 5 uM in both of their wells, mitoxantrone 5 uM in 1 of 2
and adagrasib 0.05 uM in 1 of 14. The count 21 is not itself evidence that TRIM72 is specifically
drug-responsive: the identical calling rule on 2,409 screen genes matched to TRIM72's expression level gives
a median of 17 raisers per gene (mean 18.2, p90 32), putting TRIM72's 21 at the 66.7th percentile, and
restricting to the 419 level-matched genes that are structurally incapable of readthrough spillover raises
the median to 20 and moves TRIM72 to the 53.2nd. That says TRIM72 is unremarkable among genes of its level,
not that the 21 calls are false: a no-signal null on the same design yields 0.12-0.14 calls
(median 0, P(>= 21) <= 0.0005), so the procedure is not miscalibrated.

### What moves with it

Data-defined:
- Readthrough partners. 47% of TRIM72's top 100 partners across drug-doses are readthrough-geometry genes (51% with covariates or 3 principal components removed; 41-57% across subsets), against 4.218% of all genes [upstream_coregulation/tables/a_robustness_summary.csv]. Converted to a test rather than left as a percentile -- a percentile is not a p value, which is how an earlier version read "the 99th percentile of 300 level-matched genes (median 4%) and the 92nd of 39 readthrough-exposed genes of its level (median 11%)" -- the two reference classes separate. Against the 300 expression-matched genes TRIM72 sits at the 96.7-99.0 percentile across the five variants, i.e. empirical **p 0.013-0.037** (BH over the variants q 0.028-0.037: survives). Against the 39 matched genes that are **themselves** readthrough-exposed it sits at the 84.6-94.9 percentile, i.e. **p 0.075-0.175, never below 0.05 in any variant**, on a design whose attainable floor is 1/40 = 0.025. The honest statement: TRIM72's readthrough enrichment is specific relative to expression level and typical relative to readthrough geometry -- good evidence that readthrough spillover is the right account, and direct evidence that TRIM72 is not special within it [upstream_coregulation/tables/a_null_summary.csv, rows 0-1].
- Readthrough module. TRIM72's partner count is **typical raw only**: 5,881 at |r| >= 0.3 against a matched median of 5,330 is the raw variant, and with covariates it falls to the **29th percentile** (1,405 partners against a matched median of 2,797). TRIM72's -1.08 sits at the 69th and 64th percentiles (empirical p 0.733 and 0.653) [c_program_models.csv, row 0; c_placebos.csv]. 88% and 92% of the placebos also give a negative difference [c_placebos.csv; recomputed]. Any gene that tracks drug sensitivity looks slightly 'WT-specific' against a response that only WT lines make. So the two-way p is not calibrated, and the null rests on the label permutation and the placebos.
- Power. At 80% power, the smallest detectable difference is 0.0059 log2 of program P per doubling of measured TRIM72, about 0.4%. Per SD of TRIM72, that is about 9% of the program's residual SD [c_program_models.csv, row 0: mde_diff_80, mde_diff_80_per_sd_TRIM72, sd_Y_resid]. **That is where the bound stops.** An earlier version restated it as about 0.020 log2 (1.4%) per doubling of true TRIM72, by dividing 0.00592 by a split-half reliability of 0.289, and added that it would be wider still on the replicate-well reliability of 0.114. Both restatements are withdrawn.
  - The division is not licensed. TRIM72 and every gene in program P are counted from the same cells, in the same well, against the same total-UMI denominator, so the classical correction's requirement that exposure error be independent of outcome error fails by construction; dividing amplifies the shared artefact along with the signal. Demonstrated directly: the cross-well estimate of the association is at or below zero in every arm while the same-well estimate is positive. The artefact-free version of the *estimate* needs no reliability at all -- **+0.0074 to +0.0079 program-log2 per doubling of true TRIM72 [-0.018, +0.026]**, from cov(exposure in well A, outcome in well B) over the 102 cross-batch replicate pairs.
  - The divisor was also wrong, and is group-specific: **0.301 in TP53-wild-type lines, 0.283 in TP53-altered lines**. A contrast that spans both genotypes has to be disattenuated arm by arm, not once at the difference. In the controlled re-measurement the single-divisor version of its functional-minus-knockout contrast came out 10% too large (+0.174% against the correct +0.158%), and the difference quoted here spans both groups in exactly the same way.
  - The two published anchors are three different specifications and are not interchangeable. `reliability_full_spearman_brown` 0.289317 is row 54 of `two_way/tables/trim72_reliability.csv` (`fe = ddxg`); `reliability_single_well` 0.114342 is row 111 (`fe = dd`); `key_results.csv` row 206 -- the row cited above for "0.114" -- reads **0.113639**; and row 184's `estimate` cell is 0.169124, with 0.289 appearing only in its `detail` string. Nor is there any single exchangeable-replicate reliability to quote: the two wells' residual SDs are 0.519 and 0.825, a 2.5x difference by design, one deep-plate and one shallow-plate well.
  - If one insists on dividing anyway, the answer is not a number but a range: 0.005925 / 0.301 = 0.0197 log2, i.e. **1.37%**, against about **4.0-5.3%** at the 0.105-0.080 attenuation factor the cross-well work supports -- so 0.289 is the optimistic end, and the published `mde_diff_80_attenuation_corrected` understates the division by about 3.6x [two_way/tables/trim72_reliability.csv; key_results.csv rows 184 and 206; recomputed].

### Subsets of active drug-doses (mixed; not robust)

- The 22 drug-doses where TRIM72 moves (|DD_z| >= 3, chosen on the exposure, not the outcome) give -0.0336 (CI -0.0605 to -0.0067; p 0.0175; label permutation 0.010). Holm over the 4 subsets gives 0.040 / 0.070 [genome_wide/tables/c_program_models.csv rows 7-9].
- two_way's 62 active drug-doses give -0.0300 (p 0.015; Holm over variants 0.121 / 0.144). With line effects estimated from all drug-doses, the slope falls to -0.0178 (p 0.22) [two_way/tables/key_results.csv rows 43, 126-128].
- A plain re-fit of the 22 gives -0.0155, carried by a positive altered slope (+0.0137) rather than a negative WT slope (-0.0018) [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose_line.csv; export/downstream/program_scores_per_drugdose_line.csv; recomputed].
- The 22 mix two opposite 24 h p53 states. 14 have a higher p53 program in WT, the DNA-damage drugs (idarubicin 0.5 +0.66 log2, doxorubicin 5 +0.49, idarubicin 5 +0.37). 8 have a lower one: transcription or translation inhibitors under which short-lived p53-target mRNAs fall (auranofin 5 -0.29, dinaciclib 5 -0.28, panobinostat 5 -0.26) [genome_wide/tables/b_drugdose_p53_activation.csv, program_P_delta_log2]. Pooling the two kinds can produce a negative slope by itself, and placebo genes give negative differences too.

### Broad effects (data-defined)

- Breadth. Within drug-doses, 4,500 of 16,642 genes have a common TRIM72 slope at q < 0.1 (3,213 positive). Response-matched placebos give 897-5,344 such genes (median 3,196), and expression-matched placebos a median of 5,330 [genome_wide/tables/c_calibration.csv rows 0, 41-60]. The exposure-shuffle p of 0.048 is the smallest possible with 20 shuffles [row 62]. The hits survive most covariate sets (at least 97.7% stay at p < 0.05); only the top-3-components set cuts them to 56% [c_screen_genes.csv.gz; recomputed].
- Genotype. No gene is at q < 0.1 for a WT-minus-altered slope, and the statistic is slightly deflated (lambda 0.963; 5 genes at p < 0.001 against 16.6 expected) [c_screen_genes.csv.gz; key_results.csv row 24]. A single gene would need a slope of about 0.04 log2 (about 2.7%) per doubling of TRIM72 to reach q < 0.1, about 7x the program-level bound [c_screen_genes.csv.gz, se_diff; recomputed].
- Who the partners are. The top partners are readthrough-geometry genes: 55% of the top 100 positive partners, against 4.218% of genes. Among genes at or below 10 CPM, 70.1% of exposed genes are positive partners at q < 0.1, against 21.0% of unexposed genes. None of the top genes lies within 200 kb of TRIM72 [c_screen_genes.csv.gz; recomputed]. The strongest are PDC-AS1 (+0.038) and FNDC7 (+0.141 log2 per doubling), both at q 4.8e-9. The enrichment survives adjustment for FUS and PYCARD-AS1 (50 of the top 100) [c_screen_genes.csv.gz, p_common_gw_rt; recomputed]. Each line's TRIM72 therefore tracks that line's general readthrough tendency.
- Programs. 49 of 50 atlas programs track TRIM72 across drug-doses (drug co-response). 25 do within lines (21 positive), and none differs by genotype (smallest q 0.99) [genome_wide/tables/d_program_vs_exposure.csv].
- Single cells. TRIM72-detected cells carry 9.1% more p53-score mRNA in WT lines (rate ratio 1.091, p 0.015) and 4.8% more in altered lines (1.048), plus more ATF3 (1.38x and 1.35x) [single_cell_all/tables/key_results.csv row 4; summary/T10_single_cell_all_wells.csv]. This is co-induction in the same stressed cells (list-based panel).

## Using the exported data

1. Counts are tiny. Of 62,611 usable profiles from expressing lines, 12.42% have zero UMIs and 46.4% fewer than 5. The zero fraction is reproducible only with that denominator: it is 13.69% over all 63,921 usable profiles and 16.56% over all 66,449, so always quote it with the denominator it was computed on. The median is 5 UMIs and the 90th percentile 43; the pooled level is 5.07 CPM [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; export/export_info.json]. Zero-UMI profiles score a median r2 of -1.14 and make up 52.5% of the profiles below -1 log2 [recomputed]. Pool the UMIs (umi_count / total_umi) over wells, lines or drug-doses before reading a change.
2. Plate batch. Pooled TRIM72 is 14.05 CPM on plates 1-3 and 14 against 2.09 on plates 4-13 (6.7x; 9.7x by median profile; plate 13 0.91) [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; recomputed]. Compare within a plate, as added CPM, or on the plate-6 / plate-14 pairs. Compound sets are tied to plates, and only 102 drug-doses have used wells on both kinds of plate [export/metadata/wells.csv; recomputed].
3. The DMSO reference is offset and thin.
   - TRIM72's two DMSO wells per plate sit above the drug-well median on all 14 plates (offset -0.03 to -0.89, mean -0.40 log2 over plates 1-13). That is more extreme than for 99.2% of 4,584 comparison genes, a set chosen for co-varying with TRIM72 [export/metadata/plates.csv; export/upstream/genes_per_drugdose.csv.gz; recomputed].
   - The offset belongs to the DMSO wells. The same 93 compounds give -0.06 on plate 6 and -0.47 on plate 14, and neither PYCARD-AS1 (+0.14) nor FUS (-0.08) shares it [recomputed].
   - On low plates, DMSO holds a median of 9 TRIM72 UMIs per line (2 on plates 7 and 9).
   - As a result, 84% (DESeq2) and 86% (u_dmso) of drug-doses look like lowerers, against 49% with DD_lfc [export/tahoe_deseq2/TRIM72_deseq2.csv; export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv; recomputed]. Do not build a lowerer list from u_dmso or from Tahoe's DESeq2.
4. Pick the estimand deliberately.
   - Idarubicin 5 uM is 3.61 log2 by DD_lfc, 2.91 from pooled counts against the plate's drug wells, and 2.73 from pooled counts against DMSO.
   - Dinaciclib's three doses rest on 38-59 UMIs: 1.08 / 0.69 / 1.62 pooled against 1.93 / 1.89 / 2.48 by DD_lfc -- and about 0 on an added-CPM scale, which is why the drug is now graded an artefact of the ratio scale rather than a weak raiser.
   - For homoharringtonine 5 uM, +0.37 of the corrected log2 fold comes from the composition correction. That correction assumes most genes are unchanged, which is strained after 24 h of translation block [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv; export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; recomputed].
5. Tahoe's DESeq2 file uses the same cells and DMSO wells as the export. It tracks u_dmso (r 0.81), so agreement between them validates nothing about the reference. Its median TRIM72 log2 fold is below 0 on 13 of the 14 plates (-0.06 to -0.92); **plate 9 is the exception and is positive**, and it is not an incidental one -- plate 9 carries five of the single-well raisers. An earlier version said every plate. Centre per plate before ranking [export/tahoe_deseq2/TRIM72_deseq2.csv; recomputed]. Practical traps:
   - join on drug_dose_row, not on the name: 'Erdafitinib ' and 'Selinexor ' carry trailing spaces (346 rows);
   - log2FoldChange is missing in 8.1% of rows and padj in 58.3%, clustered on low-count lines and plates;
   - 2,949 rows have fewer than 30 treated cells;
   - same-plate duplicate wells are pooled into one row (400 groups).
6. Failed and unreliable wells.
   - DESeq2 holds 569 TRIM72 rows from 15 failed wells, with a median of 5 treated cells. 341 of these rows carry a valid drug_dose_row, and all 25 of their rows at padj < 0.1 are increases. Filter on [export/metadata/wells.csv, in_atlas].
   - Plate 13 is the only source for 45 drug-doses, and its wells do not agree with reliable replicates (r -0.02).
   - Averaging DESeq2 over all plates instead of the used wells shifts 58 drug-doses; for example, topotecan 5 uM goes from 1.40 to 0.29 [export/metadata/drug_doses.csv; export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_well.csv; recomputed].
7. Replication. 1,024 of 1,132 drug-doses rest on a single well. Plate 6 and plate 14 agree at Pearson 0.775 overall, but only 0.321 without the two strong raisers (corrected from 0.75 and 0.28) [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_well.csv; recomputed]. Single-well TRIM72 values are informative only for large effects.
8. Toxic wells and small lines.
   - 94% of unusable profiles come from NCI-H596 and NCI-H2122, both TP53-altered, which leaves 17 WT and 31 altered lines in most wells [export/metadata/cell_lines.csv].
   - In the 19 toxic drug-doses, most profiles stay usable (92.3% vs 96.2%). They have 3.4x fewer cells, though, with a median of 2 vs 6 TRIM72 UMIs, and |composition_shift| exceeds 0.2 in 19.1% of profiles vs 0.7% [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; recomputed].
9. Expression filter. The cut is relative: 0.25x the median line, i.e. 0.99 CPM. NCI-H661 (0.94) is out and NCI-H2347 (1.09) is in [export/metadata/cell_lines.csv].
10. Programs and lists. P-down is exploratory: 15 genes, with the top-25 fallback used in 4 of 5 folds [export/downstream/program_flags.csv]. The curated lists under export/downstream/overlay are overlays and select nothing [export/downstream/overlay/NOTE.md].
11. Build-dependent pieces. Well order within plates changes between builds, so the seeded pieces reproduce only on an identical build: the cap-0 sample, permutation p values for same-plate duplicates, and which groups the scalar-fit cross-check picks [single_cell_all/tables/groups.csv.gz; recomputed].

## What the data leave open

- How much of TRIM72's counts, untreated and under each raiser, is readthrough from FUS -> PYCARD-AS1 rather than TRIM72's own transcript? Does the per-line PYCARD-AS1 coupling seen for idarubicin 5 uM hold for every raiser and lowerer?
- Is the plate batch a capture difference for readthrough-type RNA? PYCARD-AS1 shows a smaller version of it (2.3x vs 6.8x; plate profile r 0.92 with TRIM72), and FUS does not [export/upstream/genes_per_drugdose.csv.gz; recomputed]. If so, how much of 'untreated TRIM72' is itself a readthrough measure?
- Why do TRIM72's DMSO wells read high on every plate when PYCARD-AS1's do not?
- Is the strong-raiser response better described as a fixed added amount of RNA (30-46 CPM on both plates, per-line matched pooled counts) than as a fold? It describes the aggregate well -- observed against predicted cross-plate fold gaps correlate at 0.82-0.94 over the 92-93 pairs -- but mitoxantrone's sign flip falsifies it for individual cases, so the open part is whether ranking raisers by treated CPM would change the upstream list.
- Are TRIM72's readthrough-rich partners a consequence of FUS's high expression (about 250 CPM)? Would genes matched on the expression of their upstream neighbour show the same?
- Is the lowering by radotinib and trametinib specific to TRIM72, or part of the drug-wide fall of the readthrough index and of P24?
- Is the KMT2D association a 16p11.2 locus property, given that most of it goes with PYCARD-AS1?
- Are the negative slopes in active subsets small real effects below the 0.4%-per-doubling measured bound, or the product of pooling two opposite 24 h p53 states?
- Does genome_wide (program P and the screen) reproduce on a build with a different well order? No committed reference exists.
- How much do the seeded sensitivity results vary across seeds? Two draws give -0.060 and -0.083 for the dropped groups alone.
- Why does the line-clustered variance factor in two_way's set test move by 0.2-0.4% when every input agrees to 1e-5?
- Would the TRIM72-detection-stratified single-cell genome-wide screen (step E, not run) change the downstream picture?

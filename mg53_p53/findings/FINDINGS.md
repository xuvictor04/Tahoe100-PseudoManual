# MG53 (TRIM72) in Tahoe-100M: findings from the full run

This is a review of your full run (`mg53_setup.sh --mode full`) over Tahoe-100M: 50 pooled cancer cell lines, 379 drugs at 0.05 / 0.5 / 5 uM, 14 plates, mRNA read 24 h after treatment. Four areas were read (reproduction, upstream of MG53, downstream of MG53, exported data), and an independent verifier checked each one. Only verified claims and numbers are used here. Date: 2026-11-02.

Scope. Two questions only: what raises or lowers MG53 expression (upstream of MG53), and what MG53's level
does to p53 and its output (downstream of MG53). Whether p53 regulates MG53 is NOT in scope and is not
reported; results resting on it, including the cardiac-glycoside lead, have been removed. Separately,
'readthrough spillover' below is a fact about TRIM72's position on the chromosome (it sits 3' of FUS and
PYCARD-AS1 on the same strand, so transcription running past them is counted as TRIM72) and about the
measurement -- never a claim that anything regulates anything.

How to read the sources
- Brackets give the file, relative to `mg53_p53/` in your results, then the row, column or key. "row N" is a 0-based data row (the header is not counted). "line N" is a line of a text file. Files that exist only in the repository are marked "repository".
- "recomputed" marks a number that the review scripts derived from the table named, rather than one printed in it. The headline tables are regenerated by `mg53_p53/findings/make_tables.py` in this repository.
- Basis labels. Data-defined: genome-wide or derived from Tahoe, with no curated list. List-based: rests on a curated list (the 21-gene P53_ACTIVITY / P53_SCORE, gene_modules.csv, CollecTRI / DoRothEA regulons, curated or Tahoe MoA drug classes, the 116-gene single-cell panel, the driver-mutation list). Mixed: both.
- Units. Log2 fold change: +1 = 2x. CPM: counts per million. UMI: unique molecule count. q: Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate. A "profile" is one cell line in one well. WT: TP53 wild-type (17 lines). Altered: TP53-altered (33 lines, of which 30-32 enter the models).
- Every TRIM72-p53 relation below is an association in 24 h mRNA. Tahoe has no MG53 perturbation and no protein data.

Terms used throughout
- Readthrough: transcription that runs on past the end of a gene into the DNA 3' of it. TRIM72 sits on chr16p11.2 immediately 3' of FUS and PYCARD-AS1, on the same strand. This is a statement about position on the chromosome, not about regulation. FUS ends 17 kb upstream of TRIM72 and is far more highly expressed (252 vs about 4 CPM) [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_DoG_gene_exposure_classes.csv, row TRIM72].
- Readthrough-geometry ("sense-exposed") gene: a low-expressed gene that lies just 3' of a strongly expressed gene on the same strand, as TRIM72 does.
- Readthrough index: for each drug-dose, the level-matched rise of all readthrough-geometry genes in the genome [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_DoG_index_by_drugdose.csv].
- Placebo exposure: a gene matched to TRIM72 (on expression, or on how much it moves within lines) that is put in TRIM72's place in the same model. It shows what an unremarkable gene gives.
- DD_lfc: the atlas's corrected log2 fold change for a drug-dose. It is the median over lines of each line's log ratio against the plate's drug-well median, corrected for well position and composition. u_dmso: the uncorrected log ratio against the plate's two DMSO wells.

## Bottom line

- Reproduction (strong; computational). Your run reproduces the author's committed tables. Of 170 tables, 125 are identical, 23 close, 22 'differ' and 0 are missing, and all 12 summary tables are byte-identical [compare_with_repo.txt; summary/]. Every 'differs' traces to well order, seeded random draws, floating point, the pandas / Python version, a fresh HGNC download or one degenerate row. No short answer or headline number changes. genome_wide and export, which hold the list-free base, have no committed tables to compare against.
- What raises TRIM72 (strong for two drug-doses, moderate for the rest; data-defined). Idarubicin 5 uM and homoharringtonine 5 uM raise TRIM72 on both of their plates: 12.2x and 10.3x corrected, 6.0x and 5.4x from pooled counts [export/upstream/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv, DD_lfc; upstream_perturbations/tables/a_TRIM72_plate_batch_checks.csv, row 'significant increases: spearman corrected log2FC vs pseudocount-free pooled log2 fold']. Tahoe's own DESeq2 agrees. TRIM72 is one of 11,472 and 11,993 genes these two drugs change [upstream_perturbations/tables/c_TRIM72_specificity_in_raisers.csv]. Six single-well raisers are also supported. No lowerer is solid.
- How (strong as an association; data-defined). TRIM72 rises together with its same-strand neighbourhood 3' of FUS (readthrough spillover): PYCARD-AS1 correlates with it at r 0.72 across drug-doses [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_locus_neighbours_effects.csv]. It also tracks the genome-wide readthrough index (Spearman 0.45) [upstream_coregulation/tables/c_DoG_tests.csv]. No raiser shows TRIM72-promoter activation, but gene-level 3' counts cannot separate readthrough from promoter firing. Homoharringtonine blocks translation, so its extra mRNA cannot mean more MG53.
- Line traits and p53 (strong; data-defined). A line's untreated TRIM72 follows its own locus: PYCARD-AS1 is its top partner across lines (rho 0.82, rank 1 of 46,775 genes). TP53 status is not examined as a driver here (out of scope) [upstream_coregulation/tables/b_baseline_coexpression_genomewide.csv; b_TRIM72_baseline_tests.csv].
- Downstream p53, list-free (strong null, limited power; data-defined). Over 1,132 drug-doses x 47 lines, TRIM72 has no TP53-WT-specific association with the Tahoe-derived p53 program. The WT-minus-altered slope is -0.0018 log2 per doubling of TRIM72 (95% CI -0.0052 to +0.0016; TP53-label permutation p 0.30), in the middle of 200 placebo genes [genome_wide/tables/c_program_models.csv, row 0]. The test excludes effects above about 0.4% per doubling of measured TRIM72. Once TRIM72's measurement noise is allowed for, it excludes effects above about 1.4% per doubling of true TRIM72.
- Downstream p53, list-based (moderate; list-based). The curated 21-gene p53 score agrees on the main point: WT-minus-altered -0.0032, p 0.24 [two_way/tables/key_results.csv, row 0], and no genotype dependence in single cells. It also carries a small negative lean that the data-defined program does not share (lowest 1.8% of random 21-gene sets). Negative slopes in subsets of active drug-doses are not robust.
- Broad downstream (strong null; data-defined). Within drug-doses, 4,500 genes co-vary with TRIM72, but placebo genes give similar counts. No gene differs by TP53 genotype, and the top partners are readthrough-geometry genes [genome_wide/tables/c_calibration.csv; c_screen_genes.csv.gz]. In 24 h mRNA, TRIM72 has no detectable downstream program of its own.

### What the comparison cannot vouch for

- The tool's verdicts can mislead. It reports a row swap as a large numeric difference (the '1.5' is swapped medians). It inflates machine-precision gaps through its 1e-12 floor. It skips every .csv.gz table, and when any text column differs it stops before checking the numbers [repository setup/compare_with_repo.py lines 42-78].
- genome_wide and export commit no tables, so the Tahoe p53 program, the TRIM72-vs-every-gene screen and the export CSVs have no reference. Their inputs are checked, though:
  - the genome_wide tensor equals the atlas W1 on all 14 plates (max difference 0), W2 to 4.8e-7 over 22,118,547 values, and the committed idarubicin per-line TRIM72 to 5.4e-7 (48 lines) [genome_wide/tables/checks.csv rows 2-20];
  - the W1 check passes 42 times in the log, in three separate streaming passes [run_mg53_p53.log];
  - the exported TRIM72 values match the committed ranking for all 1,132 drug-doses (max |DD_lfc - log2FC| 5e-6) [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv vs upstream_perturbations/tables/a_TRIM72_drugdose_ranking.csv; recomputed].

  What remains unchecked across builds is the derivation step: the permutations, folds and gene selection.
- Seeded random pieces reproduce only on an identical build. The seed applied to each run's own row order recreates each run's cap-0 sample exactly (1,194 vs 1,179 groups) [single_cell_all/tables/groups.csv.gz; recomputed].
- The run spans more than one checkout. single_cell_all was rerun after the guard fix (5ad5cec), and export was rerun after a Parquet buffer overflow (843211f) [run_mg53_p53.log lines 1329 and 1913]. Neither change touches the compared tables.

### What moves with it

Data-defined:
- Readthrough partners. 47% of TRIM72's top 100 partners across drug-doses are readthrough-geometry genes (51% with covariates or 3 principal components removed; 41-57% across subsets), against 4.2% of all genes [upstream_coregulation/tables/a_robustness_summary.csv]. - Readthrough module. TRIM72 has a typical number of partners (5,881 at |r| >= 0.3 vs a matched median of 5,330) until the shared stress axes are removed. ## Downstream: what goes with more TRIM72

### p53, list-free first (data-defined)

- The read-out. The Tahoe p53 program P is 180 genes induced more in WT than in altered lines across 142 p53-activating drug-doses. It was selected genome-wide and cross-fitted [genome_wide/tables/b_p53_program.csv; key_results.csv rows 32-33].
  - Overlays support it: P recovers 19 of the 21 curated P53_ACTIVITY genes (0.23 expected, p 3.4e-36) and 35 CollecTRI TP53 targets (6.6 expected, p 3.9e-16) [genome_wide/tables/b_overlays.csv, rows 0 and 48]. Programs built from permuted labellings are empty (at most 1 gene) [b_program_stability.csv].
  - Its weak spots:
    - its TP53 specificity is partly circular;
    - in 23 of 44 selected drug-doses, a non-TP53 grouping (mostly organ) explains the WT-vs-altered divergence better [genome_wide/tables/b_feature_specificity.csv; recomputed];
    - one of 100 permuted labellings selected 135 S1 drug-doses, against 31 observed [b_program_stability.csv];
    - the 'down' arm fell back to a top-25 list in 4 of 5 folds [export/downstream/program_flags.csv].
- Primary test. Per doubling of TRIM72 within a drug-dose, program P moves +0.00040 log2 in WT lines (p 0.744) and +0.00223 in altered lines (two-way p 0.067). The WT-minus-altered difference is -0.00183 (95% CI -0.00523 to +0.00158; two-way p 0.285; TP53-label permutation p 0.304; Holm over 6 covariate sets 1.0). The model has 17 WT and 30 altered lines and 53,084 rows [genome_wide/tables/c_program_models.csv, row 0].
  - The same row holds two smaller p values for the difference: 0.046 with drug-dose-clustered errors only (p_diff_drug) and 0.027 from shuffling TRIM72 within drug-doses (p_diff_perm_within_dd). Neither allows for each line keeping its genotype across all 1,132 drug-doses, so neither is the declared test. The placebo genes below give differences more negative than TRIM72's.
  - The difference is stable across covariate sets (-0.0015 to -0.0023) and fold programs (-0.0016 to -0.0024).
  - The component slopes are not stable. With the top 3 genome-wide components as covariates, b_WT becomes -0.00248 (p 0.031); with drug-dose-only fixed effects, the difference becomes +0.00262 (p 0.47) [rows 1-6, 15-19].
- Placebos. 100 response-matched and 100 expression-matched genes, put in TRIM72's place, give median WT-minus-altered t values of -1.59 and -1.46. TRIM72's -1.08 sits at the 69th and 64th percentiles (empirical p 0.733 and 0.653) [c_program_models.csv, row 0; c_placebos.csv]. 88% and 92% of the placebos also give a negative difference [c_placebos.csv; recomputed]. Any gene that tracks drug sensitivity looks slightly 'WT-specific' against a response that only WT lines make. So the two-way p is not calibrated, and the null rests on the label permutation and the placebos.
- Power. At 80% power, the smallest detectable difference is 0.0059 log2 of program P per doubling of measured TRIM72, about 0.4%. Per SD of TRIM72, that is about 9% of the program's residual SD [c_program_models.csv, row 0: mde_diff_80, mde_diff_80_per_sd_TRIM72, sd_Y_resid]. Measured TRIM72 is noisy: its split-half reliability is 0.289 [two_way/tables/key_results.csv, row 184, detail]. For true TRIM72 the bound is therefore about 0.020 log2 (1.4%) per doubling (recomputed as 0.00592 / 0.289). It is wider still if the replicate-well reliability (0.114 [row 206]) applies.

### Broad effects (data-defined)

- Breadth. Within drug-doses, 4,500 of 16,642 genes have a common TRIM72 slope at q < 0.1 (3,213 positive). - Genotype. ## Using the exported data

1. Counts are tiny. Of 62,611 usable profiles from expressing lines, 12.4% have zero UMIs and 46.4% fewer than 5. The median is 5 UMIs and the 90th percentile 43; the pooled level is 5.07 CPM [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; export/export_info.json]. Zero-UMI profiles score a median r2 of -1.14 and make up 52.5% of the profiles below -1 log2 [recomputed]. Pool the UMIs (umi_count / total_umi) over wells, lines or drug-doses before reading a change.
2. Plate batch. Pooled TRIM72 is 14.05 CPM on plates 1-3 and 14 against 2.09 on plates 4-13 (6.7x; 9.7x by median profile; plate 13 0.91) [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; recomputed]. Compare within a plate, as added CPM, or on the plate-6 / plate-14 pairs. Compound sets are tied to plates, and only 102 drug-doses have used wells on both kinds of plate [export/metadata/wells.csv; recomputed].
3. The DMSO reference is offset and thin.
   - TRIM72's two DMSO wells per plate sit above the drug-well median on all 14 plates (offset -0.03 to -0.89, mean -0.40 log2 over plates 1-13). That is more extreme than for 99.2% of 4,584 comparison genes, a set chosen for co-varying with TRIM72 [export/metadata/plates.csv; export/upstream/genes_per_drugdose.csv.gz; recomputed].
   - The offset belongs to the DMSO wells. The same 93 compounds give -0.06 on plate 6 and -0.47 on plate 14, and neither PYCARD-AS1 (+0.14) nor FUS (-0.08) shares it [recomputed].
   - On low plates, DMSO holds a median of 9 TRIM72 UMIs per line (2 on plates 7 and 9).
   - As a result, 84% (DESeq2) and 86% (u_dmso) of drug-doses look like lowerers, against 49% with DD_lfc [export/tahoe_deseq2/TRIM72_deseq2.csv; export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv; recomputed]. Do not build a lowerer list from u_dmso or from Tahoe's DESeq2.
4. Pick the estimand deliberately.
   - Idarubicin 5 uM is 3.61 log2 by DD_lfc, 2.91 from pooled counts against the plate's drug wells, and 2.73 from pooled counts against DMSO.
   - Dinaciclib's three doses rest on 38-59 UMIs: 1.08 / 0.69 / 1.62 pooled against 1.93 / 1.89 / 2.48 by DD_lfc.
   - For homoharringtonine 5 uM, +0.37 of the corrected log2 fold comes from the composition correction. That correction assumes most genes are unchanged, which is strained after 24 h of translation block [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_drugdose.csv; export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; recomputed].
5. Tahoe's DESeq2 file uses the same cells and DMSO wells as the export. It tracks u_dmso (r 0.81), so agreement between them validates nothing about the reference. Its median TRIM72 log2 fold is below 0 on every plate (-0.06 to -0.92), so centre it per plate before ranking [export/tahoe_deseq2/TRIM72_deseq2.csv; recomputed]. Practical traps:
   - join on drug_dose_row, not on the name: 'Erdafitinib ' and 'Selinexor ' carry trailing spaces (346 rows);
   - log2FoldChange is missing in 8.1% of rows and padj in 58.3%, clustered on low-count lines and plates;
   - 2,949 rows have fewer than 30 treated cells;
   - same-plate duplicate wells are pooled into one row (400 groups).
6. Failed and unreliable wells.
   - DESeq2 holds 569 TRIM72 rows from 15 failed wells, with a median of 5 treated cells. 341 of these rows carry a valid drug_dose_row, and all 25 of their rows at padj < 0.1 are increases. Filter on [export/metadata/wells.csv, in_atlas].
   - Plate 13 is the only source for 45 drug-doses, and its wells do not agree with reliable replicates (r -0.02).
   - Averaging DESeq2 over all plates instead of the used wells shifts 58 drug-doses; for example, topotecan 5 uM goes from 1.40 to 0.29 [export/metadata/drug_doses.csv; export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_well.csv; recomputed].
7. Replication. 1,024 of 1,132 drug-doses rest on a single well. Plate 6 and plate 14 agree at r 0.75 overall, but only 0.28 without the two strong raisers [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_well.csv; recomputed]. Single-well TRIM72 values are informative only for large effects.
8. Toxic wells and small lines.
   - 94% of unusable profiles come from NCI-H596 and NCI-H2122, both TP53-altered, which leaves 17 WT and 31 altered lines in most wells [export/metadata/cell_lines.csv].
   - In the 19 toxic drug-doses, most profiles stay usable (92.3% vs 96.2%). They have 3.4x fewer cells, though, with a median of 2 vs 6 TRIM72 UMIs, and |composition_shift| exceeds 0.2 in 19.1% of profiles vs 0.7% [export/TRIM72/TRIM72_per_profile.csv; recomputed].
9. Expression filter. The cut is relative: 0.25x the median line, i.e. 0.99 CPM. NCI-H661 (0.94) is out and NCI-H2347 (1.09) is in [export/metadata/cell_lines.csv].
10. Programs and lists. P-down is exploratory: 15 genes, with the top-25 fallback used in 4 of 5 folds [export/downstream/program_flags.csv]. The curated lists under export/downstream/overlay are overlays and select nothing [export/downstream/overlay/NOTE.md].
11. Build-dependent pieces. Well order within plates changes between builds, so the seeded pieces reproduce only on an identical build: the cap-0 sample, permutation p values for same-plate duplicates, and which groups the scalar-fit cross-check picks [single_cell_all/tables/groups.csv.gz; recomputed].

## What the data leave open

- How much of TRIM72's counts, untreated and under each raiser, is readthrough from FUS -> PYCARD-AS1 rather than TRIM72's own transcript? Does the per-line PYCARD-AS1 coupling seen for idarubicin 5 uM hold for every raiser and lowerer?
- Is the plate batch a capture difference for readthrough-type RNA? PYCARD-AS1 shows a smaller version of it (2.3x vs 6.8x; plate profile r 0.92 with TRIM72), and FUS does not [export/upstream/genes_per_drugdose.csv.gz; recomputed]. If so, how much of 'untreated TRIM72' is itself a readthrough measure?
- Why do TRIM72's DMSO wells read high on every plate when PYCARD-AS1's do not?
- Is the strong-raiser response better described as a fixed added amount of RNA (43-61 CPM on both plates) than as a fold? Would ranking raisers by treated CPM change the upstream list?
- Are TRIM72's readthrough-rich partners a consequence of FUS's high expression (about 250 CPM)? Would genes matched on the expression of their upstream neighbour show the same?
- Is the lowering by radotinib and trametinib specific to TRIM72, or part of the drug-wide fall of the readthrough index and of P24?
- Is the KMT2D association a 16p11.2 locus property, given that most of it goes with PYCARD-AS1?
- Are the negative slopes in active subsets small real effects below the 1-2% bound, or the product of pooling two opposite 24 h p53 states?
- Does genome_wide (program P and the screen) reproduce on a build with a different well order? No committed reference exists.
- How much do the seeded sensitivity results vary across seeds? Two draws give -0.060 and -0.083 for the dropped groups alone.
- Why does the line-clustered variance factor in two_way's set test move by 0.2-0.4% when every input agrees to 1e-5?
- Would the TRIM72-detection-stratified single-cell genome-wide screen (step E, not run) change the downstream picture?

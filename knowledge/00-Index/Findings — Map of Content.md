# Findings — Map of Content

What the study concluded, and how much of each conclusion survived its own controls.

## Start with the answer

**[[What this study found]]** — both questions, their effect sizes with intervals, and the limits. If you
read one note, read that one.

## The two questions

| Note | The question | The short answer |
|---|---|---|
| [[Upstream of MG53]] | what raises or lowers MG53's expression | its own genomic neighbourhood, more than any regulator; only 2 of 21 raisers replicate; the muscle factors that drive it natively are absent here |
| [[Downstream of MG53]] | what MG53's level does to p53 and p53's output | nothing detectable in wild-type lines; the raw positive correlation is drug identity; per gene the association points both ways equally |

## The central measurement

**[[The measurement with controls]]** — the one number the study was built to produce: p53 activity change
per MG53 change, in the 11 lines with functional p53, against a control arm of 10 lines that have no intact
TP53 allele and therefore cannot mount a p53 response. +0.095% per doubling (CI −0.161% to +0.352%), against
a detection limit of +0.357%.

## The ledger

**[[Corrections and retractions]]** — every number that was published and later corrected, with both values
and the reason. Mostly anti-conservative intervals replaced by conservative ones, a null replaced by a
calibrated null, and one reference family that turned out to be a discovery set.

This is not an appendix. Reading it is the fastest way to judge how much weight the rest will bear.

## What would change the answer

Stated in the notes themselves, collected here:

- **Per-line values for arbitrary genes across all 1,132 exposures.** Only MG53 and the programme score are
  resolved per line for every exposure; five named genes are resolved over 18 selected exposures. An
  `export/all_genes` per-line addition would make the per-gene contrasts testable at the unit where the
  interesting signal would live. ([[Downstream of MG53]], [[MDM2 and MG53]])
- **More than 17 wild-type lines.** Several tests are power-limited rather than null — the
  wild-type-versus-altered difference in the screen has about 21% power against a fully p53-mediated effect.
  ([[The measurement with controls]])
- **A replicate well for the other 17 raisers.** 1,024 of 1,132 drug-doses sit in a single well, so most of
  the upstream result cannot be replicated within the dataset at all. ([[Upstream of MG53]])
- **Anything at the protein level.** MG53's known biology is post-translational and Tahoe has no protein
  measurement, so the cleanest version of the question cannot be asked here. ([[MG53 (TRIM72)]])

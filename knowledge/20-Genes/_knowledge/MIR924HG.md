> [!summary] In one line
> MIR924HG is a long non-coding RNA on chromosome 18, named for the microRNA encoded within it, and almost nothing about it is established.

## What it is

The gene is an annotation and a coordinate. Also catalogued as LINC00669, it is an intergenic non-coding transcript on chromosome 18 whose only named feature is that it hosts miR-924. Reference databases list no molecular function and no pathway membership, and expression is low wherever surveyed. A single report in nasopharyngeal carcinoma proposes a pro-proliferative role acting on SOCS1; I cannot vouch for its details, and it has no evident replication. Whether the host transcript does anything of its own, rather than serving as the precursor from which miR-924 is excised, has not been addressed.

## Why it appears in this work

It is here only as a member of the repression arm of [[p53 program P]] — an assignment made from expression behaviour, not from curated biology.

## What is uncertain

Essentially everything, starting with the measurement. A microRNA host transcript is cleaved and degraded as part of its own processing, so reads assigned to it reflect precursor turnover as much as transcription, and a 3'-biased assay quantifies a low-abundance nuclear lncRNA poorly. At this abundance ambient RNA alone can move the number. With no established function there is no mechanism against which the repression-arm assignment could be checked, and nothing separates real p53-dependent repression from the covariates in [[Why MG53 is hard to measure here]].
